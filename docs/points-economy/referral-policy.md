# DOUDO Referral Policy

Referral rewards are based only on `PURCHASE` points minted from settled fiat payments.

Reward points from bundles, promotions, collection books, refunds, or manual adjustments do not qualify for referral rewards.

## Launch Rules

- Reward only after the referred user's payment is settled.
- Cap referral rewards below expected gross margin.
- Do not reward self-referrals, same-wallet loops, or wallets with recent chargeback flags.
- Hold suspicious rewards for manual review instead of minting first and clawing back later.

## Review Triggers

- More than 5 referred wallets share payment instruments, device fingerprints, or withdrawal destinations.
- Referral rewards exceed 20% of the referrer's paid purchase volume in 30 days.
- Any wallet in the referral path has more than 1 chargeback in 30 days.
